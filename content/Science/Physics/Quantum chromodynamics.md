- Non-Abelian (non-commutative) [[Gauge theory]] with symmetry group SU(3) that describes strong force between [[Quark]] mediated by [[Gluon]]
	- Can be represented by 3D color space with axes of red, green, and blue
	- Because it is non-commutative, order of quark color changes matters, so gluons must carry color charge
	- Ex. suppose a green-antiblue gluon and a red-antigreen gluon fly toward a blue quark
		- Absorb green-antiblue gluon first, then red-antigreen gluon
			1. Blue quark becomes green quark
			2. Green quark becomes red quark
		- Absorb red-antigreen gluon first, then green-antiblue gluon
			1. Blue quark cannot absorb red-antigreen gluon
- [[Color charge]] is the analog of electric charge
	- Red, green, blue
- Force carrier: [[Gluon]]
- Three major properties
	- [[Color confinement]]: particles with [[Color charge]] cannot be isolated
		- Results in color neutrality: particles must combine to look white (neutral)
	- [[Asymptotic freedom]]: interactions become asymptotically weaker (approach zero) as energy scale increases and length scale decreases
	- [[Chiral symmetry breaking]]: quarks can switch between left-handed and right-handed chiralities because the gluon field fills space with quark condensate
